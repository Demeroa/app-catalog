Tears of Stars — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Tears of Stars is a tarot reading app for personal reflection. It does not require an account, does not show advertising, and does not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app has no user accounts and no server that receives your readings, choices, or settings.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your selected language, sound preference, welcome-guide status, recent reading history, and daily-reading state on your device so it can remember your choices. This information stays on the device and is removed when you delete the app.

Optional purchases

On iOS, optional supporter purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive payment details and do not keep a record of who made a purchase or how much they paid. Supporter purchases do not unlock content or features.

Sharing

If you choose Share, iOS opens its system share sheet. The app sends content only to the app or service that you select; that destination's privacy policy then applies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact kospi7770@gmail.com.
